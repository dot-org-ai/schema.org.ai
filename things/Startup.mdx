# Startup

A Startup is a startup company with an explicit lifecycle stage — `idea`, `validating`, `building`, `scaling`, or `established` — plus its elevator pitch, industry, founding date, and the [ICP](ICP.mdx)s it targets.

In the G1–G5 ladder, a Startup is the unit a venue mints (G4: brand + offer stamped onto an abstract artifact) and tenants operate (G5). It is the flagship native type of the vocabulary and a live atlas emission.
