# EvidenceRegister

The EvidenceRegister is the **tier-governed** half of the two-register logging law (R13-1): *every metered event writes to two registers.* Its counterpart is the cost-governed [ObservabilityRegister](ObservabilityRegister.mdx).

## Definition

- **Append-only objects under retention hold** — deletion is structurally unavailable, a [DoctrineWall](DoctrineWall.mdx), not a policy. (Table-format immutability, where deletes are supported verbs you promise not to use, is mitigation — it does not satisfy this definition; object-lock does.)
- **Retained at least as long as the longest open dispute window.** Retention derives from the [AssuranceTier](AssuranceTier.mdx), never from storage cost. An oracle whose evidence expires before the dispute right lapses is **self-annulling**.
- **Counterparty-checkable** — shared reconciliation or signed receipts; the strongest form is confirmable by the party on the other side of the transaction, not merely self-attested.
- **The evidence is the object, never the table.** Any catalog above the objects (a queryable table format, a metadata catalog) is a *view* with **zero evidentiary status** — a table delete hides, it cannot destroy.

## Event vocabulary

The register's event vocabulary is seeded by [GS1 EPCIS](https://standards.org.ai/GS1/EPCIS) — the what/when/where/why evidence-event standard. (Lexeme-binding note: EPCIS `TransformationEvent` is a supply-chain event type and is **not** the canon verb `transform`; the two bind separately.)

## Role in assurance

- The evidence ledger is a member of the T2 commerce pack — every mint that sells carries one from birth.
- Demanded evidence under R12-2 (mitigating controls become evidence) lands here.
- Duty preservation: retention runs even while the business sits in its [MinimalRiskCondition](MinimalRiskCondition.mdx).
