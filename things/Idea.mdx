# Idea

An Idea is a business concept: a name, an elevator pitch ("TodoAI uses AI to prioritize your tasks automatically"), an optional longer description, and a lifecycle status from raw onward. Ideas are validated through their [Hypothesis](Hypothesis.mdx) list — evidence, not conviction — on the way to becoming a [Startup](Startup.mdx).
