# Offer

An Offer is what a platform business puts in front of a buyer: the packaged promise — what is delivered, to whom (ICP coordinates), how (delivery mode), and on what pricing structure.

## The bucket ruling

**Offer is an ADMITTED EXTENSION**: `subClassOf` [schema:Offer](https://schema.org/Offer), ruled by Nathan's F5 approval (org.ai issue #11, 2026-07-18): *"Product, Service, Offer = extension (subClassOf schema:Product / schema:Service / schema:Offer)"* — org.ai ADR 0004 Q2: every offer of ours is truthfully a `schema:Offer` (a proposal to transfer rights / provide a service), and our shape adds permanent structure. Crawlers walking the subclass edge still understand the parent type; the term is schema.org.ai-homed.
