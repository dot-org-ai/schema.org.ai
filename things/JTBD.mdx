# JTBD

A JTBD (Job To Be Done) captures a job the customer is trying to accomplish using the **When… / I want to… / So I can…** pattern:

- **situation** — *When* I have too many tasks
- **motivation** — *I want to* find the most important one
- **outcome** — *So I can* make meaningful progress

Jobs can additionally be scored on their functional, emotional, and social dimensions, prioritized (1–5), and tagged with how often they occur. A JTBD typically belongs to an [ICP](ICP.mdx) persona.
