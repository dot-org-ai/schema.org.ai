# SeamClass

SeamClass classifies a seam where a business verb crosses the membrane. The seam class determines how the verb prices on the [AssuranceTier](AssuranceTier.mdx) axis and where the human signature (if any) sits.

## Classes

| Class | $id | Definition |
|-------|-----|------------|
| Attestation | `https://schema.org.ai/SeamClass/Attestation` | The business states something others rely on — data, grades, benchmarks. Assurance rides on the statement's evidence; where others allocate on the attestation, the tier rises with the reliance (an attestation others allocate on prices at T2). |
| TransactionInRegime | `https://schema.org.ai/SeamClass/TransactionInRegime` | Bounded consideration exchanged against a signed Offer with an external financial adjudicator. Prices at T2 per the R12-1 settlement carve-out: the T3 floor binds deployment of capital, not settlement of consideration. |
| CovenantVerb | `https://schema.org.ai/SeamClass/CovenantVerb` | A human signs a ceiling once; the machine mints narrowing children within it. The T3 moment lives here — at the envelope-mint covenant — not at each settlement. |
| NeverMintWall | `https://schema.org.ai/SeamClass/NeverMintWall` | A verb the estate never mints — a [DoctrineWall](DoctrineWall.mdx) in seam position (e.g. no-custody-ever). Removal is a re-tiering event. |

## Notes

- These four classes have been **independently re-derived** by builders who had never seen this vocabulary (recorded as pre-binding recurrence, 2026-07-19) — evidence they carve the seam space at its joints.
- Seam class and tier compose: a reversible TransactionInRegime can sit at T1; the same verb selling under a signed Offer is ≥T2; the covenant that arms it is the T3 object.
- A conduit-performer seam (per-verb L2 whose approval edge is exogenous — both ends outside the membrane, e.g. client→practitioner power-of-attorney and practitioner→regulator duties) is metered but not authorized by the membrane: **the membrane meters; it does not authorize.**
