# Service

A Service is work provided to a buyer as an ongoing or on-demand engagement — including the platform's services-as-software: services delivered by agents with software economics.

## The bucket ruling

**Service is an ADMITTED EXTENSION**: `subClassOf` [schema:Service](https://schema.org/Service), ruled by Nathan's F5 approval (org.ai issue #11, 2026-07-18): *"Product, Service, Offer = extension (subClassOf schema:Product / schema:Service / schema:Offer)"* — org.ai ADR 0004 Q2: every service of ours is truthfully a `schema:Service`, and our shape adds permanent structure. Crawlers walking the subclass edge still understand the parent type; the term is schema.org.ai-homed.
