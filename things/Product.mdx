# Product

A Product is something the platform's businesses make, sell, or use — spanning instance-grain products and the standards-seeded (UNSPSC) product-category taxonomy the atlas carries.

## The bucket ruling

**Product is an ADMITTED EXTENSION**: `subClassOf` [schema:Product](https://schema.org/Product), ruled by Nathan's F5 approval (org.ai issue #11, 2026-07-18): *"Product, Service, Offer = extension (subClassOf schema:Product / schema:Service / schema:Offer)"* — org.ai ADR 0004 Q2: every instance of ours is truthfully an instance of theirs, with our shape adding permanent structure. Category-grain nodes (the UNSPSC-seeded Lumber *category*) are tolerated under the parent per ADR 0004: *"Grain divergence is tolerable; falsity is not"* — the wild also uses `schema:Product` category-ish.

Crawlers walking the subclass edge still understand the parent type; the term is schema.org.ai-homed.
