# Market

A Market is the competitive landscape addressing a particular [Problem](Problem.mdx) or [Opportunity](Opportunity.mdx) — the typed field of incumbents, substitutes, and status-quo workarounds.

Distinct from [schema:Place](https://schema.org/Place) (geographic). A Market in the canonical sense is *a competitive landscape*, not a geographic region. "The US bookkeeping software market" is a competitive landscape; "the Pacific Northwest" is a Place.

Markets carry the *Position* axis: the differentiated quadrants that competitors occupy. A 2×2 with one axis being "AI-native vs incumbent" and another being "vertical vs horizontal" is one common Position framework.

## Properties

| Property | Type | Description | Inherited From |
|----------|------|-------------|----------------|
| `$type` | Text | Always "Market" | - |
| `name` | Text | Market name (e.g. "Small Business Bookkeeping Software Market") | Thing |
| `description` | Text | What the Market addresses | Thing |
| `addresses` | Problem | The Problem this Market is structured around | Market |
| `incumbents` | Organization[] | Established players | Market |
| `entrants` | Organization[] | Newer entrants challenging incumbents | Market |
| `statusQuo` | Text | How the ICP solves the problem WITHOUT a vendor (manual, in-house) | Market |
| `tam` | Text | Total addressable market | Market |
| `growthRate` | Text | Annual growth | Market |
| `positions` | Text[] | Differentiated competitive positions (e.g. "vertical-AI-native", "horizontal-incumbent") | Market |
| `disruptionScore` | Number | 0.0–1.0 — how AI-disruptable the Market is | Market |

## Examples

### Example 1: Small business bookkeeping software market

```typescript
import type { Market } from 'schema.org.ai'

const sbBookkeepingMarket: Market = {
  $type: 'Market',
  $id: 'https://markets.org.ai/Small_Business_Bookkeeping_Software',
  name: 'Small Business Bookkeeping Software Market',
  description: 'Vendors selling bookkeeping and accounting software to small businesses (< 100 employees) in North America.',
  addresses: 'https://problems.org.ai/Manual_Bookkeeping_Burden',
  incumbents: ['Intuit (QuickBooks)', 'Sage', 'Xero', 'FreshBooks'],
  entrants: ['Pilot', 'Bench', 'Botkeeper', 'LedgerLoop'],
  statusQuo: 'Spreadsheets + manual paper-checking by an in-house or contracted bookkeeper',
  tam: '$24B (US small business bookkeeping spend)',
  growthRate: '6-8% annually',
  positions: [
    'Horizontal incumbent (QuickBooks)',
    'Horizontal challenger (Xero)',
    'AI-native service-as-software (Pilot, Bench)',
    'Vertical AI service-as-software (LedgerLoop for accounting firms)',
  ],
  disruptionScore: 0.85,
}
```
