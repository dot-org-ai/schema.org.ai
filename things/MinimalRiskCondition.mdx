# MinimalRiskCondition

A MinimalRiskCondition (MRC) is the **machine-reachable, duty-preserving business halt** a system enters when it exits its [DeclaredScope](DeclaredScope.mdx). Derived from [SAE J3016](https://standards.org.ai/SAE/J3016)'s minimal risk condition — the safe state an automated vehicle achieves when it cannot continue — carried to business autonomy: the halt must preserve **duties** (obligations, retention, dispute rights), not merely stop activity.

An [AutonomyClaim](AutonomyClaim.mdx) at [L4](AutonomyLevel.mdx) is only honest if its MRC is reachable by the machine itself, without human cooperation, and lawful while degraded.

## lawfulDegradedState — required, Frozen | Flat

Every MRC **must declare** its `lawfulDegradedState`:

- **`Frozen`** — the system de-arms and holds state; exposure persists but nothing new is created; all obligations (retention, dispute, covenant duties) remain live and serviced. A kill switch that de-arms but does not close positions is *Frozen*, and must be declared as such.
- **`Flat`** — the system closes exposure to zero before halting.

Declaring which of the two the MRC reaches is part of tier assignment: every MRC declares Frozen-or-Flat (Law 2), and an undeclared degraded state is an unassigned hazard.

## Duty preservation

- Per A2 (obligations carry tiers), a business holding T3 obligations is a T3 process **even while halted** — the MRC must keep servicing tier-bearing obligations. **The covenant registry is the living will: the T3 MRC is a query over it.**
- Evidence duties survive the halt: retention on the [EvidenceRegister](EvidenceRegister.mdx) runs at least as long as the longest open dispute window, halted or not.
- A degraded-but-lawful MRC is a member of the T2 commerce pack — e.g. refusing service *before* debiting (503-before-debit), or dropping to a read-only degraded state.
