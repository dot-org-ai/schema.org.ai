# Company

A Company is a concrete firm — an actual organization that operates in an [Industry](Industry.mdx), instantiates a [CompanyType](CompanyType.mdx) archetype, employs workers, and buys and sells. It is the platform's firm-grain noun; "business" as a word stays in the Register-1 thesis vocabulary and resolves here (Business is **not admitted** as a type — Company carries the referent).

## The bucket ruling

**Company is an ADMITTED EXTENSION**: `subClassOf` [schema:Organization](https://schema.org/Organization), ruled by Nathan's F5 approval (org.ai issue #11, 2026-07-18): *"Company = extension (subClassOf schema:Organization, per the R3 exemplar)"*. This is the ADR 0002 R3 exemplar move — *"schema.org has no Company; the clean term is ours to mint"* — restated by org.ai ADR 0004 Q2 (every company is truthfully an organization; our shape adds permanent structure). Crawlers walking the subclass edge still understand the parent type.
