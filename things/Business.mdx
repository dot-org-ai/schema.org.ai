# Business

A business entity expressed as **Business-as-Code** — its agents, processes, KPIs, financial rails, and workflows codified as a runnable system. The unifying type for AI-native ventures where the business itself is software: definitions of *who* (agents, roles), *what* (offerings, products), *how* (processes, workflows), and *measured by* (goals, KPIs) live as typed code, not in spreadsheets or wikis.

Distinct from schema:Organization (which describes an organizational entity from the outside) by carrying the runtime artifacts that make the business autonomous-capable.

## Properties

| Property | Type | Description | Inherited From |
|----------|------|-------------|----------------|
| `$type` | Text | Always "Business" | - |
| `name` | Text | Business name | Thing |
| `description` | Text | What the business does | Thing |

| `businessType` | CompanyType | Industry/model archetype (e.g. AccountingFirm) | Business |
| `agents` | Agent[] | Roster of agents employed by the business | Business |
| `processes` | Process[] | Codified business processes | Business |
| `workflows` | Workflow[] | Multi-step automations | Business |
| `kpis` | Goal[] | Measured outcomes (revenue, retention, etc.) | Business |
| `offerings` | Service[] / Product[] | What the business sells | Business |
| `financialRails` | Text | Payment/billing infrastructure (e.g. "Stripe Connect") | Business |
| `digital` | Number | 0.8 by default (varies per business's automation level) | Thing |

## Subtypes

- **AutonomousBusiness**: A business where the entire operational stack is agent-driven (no human in core loops).
- **AIOperatedBusiness**: A business with significant agentic automation but humans in oversight roles.

## Examples

### Example 1: Solo AI-operated business

```typescript
import type { Business } from 'schema.org.ai'

const ledgerLoop: Business = {
  $type: 'Business',
  $id: 'https://schema.org.ai/Business/LedgerLoop',
  name: 'Ledger Loop',
  description: 'AI bookkeeping for accounting firms.',
  url: 'https://ledgerloop.svcs.st',
  businessType: 'https://schema.org.ai/CompanyType/AccountingFirm',
  agents: [
    'https://schema.org.ai/Agent/Sally-AIBookkeeper',
    'https://schema.org.ai/Agent/Quinn-AccountManager',
  ],
  processes: [
    'https://schema.org.ai/Process/ReconcileBankStatements',
    'https://schema.org.ai/Process/CategorizeTransactions',
  ],
  workflows: ['https://schema.org.ai/Workflow/MonthlyClose'],
  kpis: [
    { $type: 'Goal', name: 'MRR', target: 100000 },
    { $type: 'Goal', name: 'Customer retention', target: 0.95 },
  ],
  offerings: ['https://schema.org.ai/Service/Bookkeeping'],
  financialRails: 'Stripe Connect',
  digital: 0.95,
}
```

```typescript
const transformingFirm: Business = {

}
```
