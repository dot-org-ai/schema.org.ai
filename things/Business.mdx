# Business — NOT ADMITTED

**Business is not admitted to the schema.org.ai profile.** Ruled by Nathan on org.ai issue #11 (2026-07-18), verbatim:

> **Business = not admitted** (Company carries the referent; 'business' stays the Register-1 thesis word).

There is no `schema.org.ai/Business` class, no `Business` context term, and no borrow (schema.org defines no Business type). The formerly-native Business class was withdrawn by this ruling; its referent — a concrete firm expressed as Business-as-Code — is carried by **[Company](Company.mdx)** (`subClassOf` [schema:Organization](https://schema.org/Organization), the ADR 0002 R3 exemplar extension).

"Business" as a *word* remains the Register-1 thesis vocabulary (business-as-code, autonomous businesses) — prose, not a type.

## Use instead

- **[Company](Company.mdx)** — the concrete firm
- **[CompanyType](CompanyType.mdx)** — the firm archetype
- **[Startup](Startup.mdx)** — the venue-minted company with lifecycle stage
