# Thesis

A Thesis is the strategic bet a startup is founded on — the approach coupled with a [Problem](Problem.mdx) to scope an opportunity (Service-as-Software, Agent, Headless SaaS are canonical theses). It is a cascade noun: recurrence-proven and live-emitted by the atlas.

## The bucket ruling

**Thesis is NATIVE**, ruled by Nathan on org.ai issue #11 (2026-07-18): *"Problem, Thesis → native now (recurrence-proven, live-emitted)"*.

Because the name collides with [`schema.org/Thesis`](https://schema.org/Thesis) — *"a thesis or dissertation document submitted in support of candidature for an academic degree"*, a different referent sharing the name — the shadow mechanics of org.ai ADR 0004 Q3 apply mechanically: the bare context term `Thesis` binds to `https://schema.org.ai/Thesis`; schema.org's dissertation sense stays reachable by its full IRI (or `schema:Thesis`), never re-homed.

Distinct from [InvestmentThesis](InvestmentThesis.mdx) (the fund-side investment-selection document).
