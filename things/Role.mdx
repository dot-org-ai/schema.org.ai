# Role

A Role is a job role definition: a named position or function with the skills it requires, the responsibilities it carries, the permissions and tools it is granted, what it can handle, delegate, and approve, whom it escalates and reports to, and the preferred worker type (`agent` or `human`) to fill it.

Per the ADR 0002 R3 bucket ruling, Role is **native and shadows**: [schema.org/Role](https://schema.org/Role) is a reified edge-qualifier (a way of annotating a relationship between two other things) — a different concept that happens to share the name. The context term `Role` maps to this type; schema.org's Role stays reachable by its full IRI.
