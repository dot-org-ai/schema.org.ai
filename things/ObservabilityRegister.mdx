# ObservabilityRegister

The ObservabilityRegister is the **cost-governed** half of the two-register logging law (R13-1): *every metered event writes to two registers.* Its counterpart is the tier-governed [EvidenceRegister](EvidenceRegister.mdx).

## Definition

- **Expirable** — data ages out on a hot window sized by cost, not by duty.
- **Sampled** — completeness is a budget decision.
- **Mutable** — rows can be rewritten, compacted, or dropped; nothing here carries evidentiary status.

The register exists for operations: debugging, dashboards, latency analysis, capacity planning. It is governed by cost because none of its guarantees are load-bearing for disputes.

## The split is the point

Statements like "we keep full request bodies to help with disputes" and "bodies expire after N days" are contradictory **unless** expiry ≥ the dispute window — which no cost-governed store should promise. The two-register law resolves the contradiction structurally: **expiry is a property of the observability register only**; anything a dispute can turn on is written to the [EvidenceRegister](EvidenceRegister.mdx), whose retention derives from tier. Neither register can be argued into doing the other's job.
