# Law

A Law is a **thin normative venue commitment**: a ratified statement, published in a venue's canon, that binds that venue to definitions it does not own.

## The register architecture

**Define once in the open register; legislate thin in the venue. Laws cite, never redefine.**

- Foundational concepts live in the open register (this vocabulary) with their FULL definitional content — delta tables, assignment functions, floors.
- A Law carries only the venue's normative commitment, referencing every concept by fully-scoped `$id` (`https://schema.org.ai/AutonomyLevel/L4`, never "L4").
- A Law that restates a definition has forked it — the same disease-class as vendored duplicate primitives. Nothing exists twice.

## Not schema:Legislation

This type is deliberately **not** subclassed from `schema:Legislation`. Legislation carries the civil-statute register — acts of public legislatures. A canon Law is a venue commitment; claiming the Legislation lineage would be a false-register claim, so the type is minted natively under CreativeWork.
