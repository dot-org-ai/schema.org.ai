# Team

A Team is a named group of workers — agents and humans together — with a member roster, an optional lead, shared goals, communication channels, and shared context.

Per the ADR 0002 R3 bucket ruling, Team is an **admitted extension**: `subClassOf` [schema:Organization](https://schema.org/Organization), because schema.org has no generic Team type. Crawlers walking the subclass edge still understand the parent type.
