# Hypothesis

A Hypothesis is a testable assumption that can be validated or invalidated: a statement ("Users will pay $10/month for AI task prioritization"), the metric it moves (`conversion_rate`), the target value to hit, the current testing status, and the evidence collected along the way.

Hypotheses are the validation units behind an [Idea](Idea.mdx) — an idea graduates by having its hypotheses tested against real evidence, not by conviction.
