# Department

A Department is a functional grouping within a [CompanyType](CompanyType.mdx) or [Business](Business.mdx) — Tax, Audit, Engineering, Sales, Customer Success, Operations, Finance, HR, Legal, Marketing. Departments group related [JobTypes](JobType.mdx) and [Tasks](Task.mdx) by what they accomplish.

Subtype of schema:Organization because departments are sub-organizations within a larger Organization. Each Department has its own JobTypes, processes, and KPIs but operates within the parent's governance and strategy.

## Properties

| Property | Type | Description | Inherited From |
|----------|------|-------------|----------------|
| `$type` | Text | Always "Department" | - |
| `name` | Text | Department name (e.g. "Tax") | Thing |
| `description` | Text | What this department does | Thing |
| `parentOrganization` | Organization | Parent Business or CompanyType | Department |
| `jobTypes` | JobType[] | Roles within this department | Department |
| `processes` | Process[] | Business processes owned by this department | Department |
| `tasks` | Task[] | Tasks performed by this department | Department |
| `kpis` | Goal[] | Department-specific KPIs | Department |
| `head` | JobType | Department head role | Department |

## Examples

### Example 1: Tax department at an Accounting Firm

```typescript
import type { Department } from 'schema.org.ai'

const taxDept: Department = {
  $type: 'Department',
  $id: 'https://departments.org.ai/Tax',
  name: 'Tax',
  description: 'Department responsible for client tax preparation, planning, and compliance.',
  parentOrganization: 'https://companytypes.org.ai/Accounting_Firm',
  jobTypes: [
    'https://jobtypes.org.ai/Tax_Manager',
    'https://jobtypes.org.ai/Tax_Senior',
    'https://jobtypes.org.ai/Tax_Staff',
  ],
  processes: [
    'https://processes.org.ai/Prepare_Individual_Tax_Returns',
    'https://processes.org.ai/Quarterly_Estimated_Payments',
  ],
  head: 'https://jobtypes.org.ai/Tax_Partner',
}
```
