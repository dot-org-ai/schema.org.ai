# Company

Ours, and a kind of schema.org’s.

    https://schema.org.ai/Company

A concrete firm: an organization that operates in an Industry, instantiates a CompanyType archetype, employs workers, and buys and sells. ADMITTED EXTENSION per Nathan's F5 ruling (org.ai#11, 2026-07-18): 'Company = extension (subClassOf schema:Organization, per the R3 exemplar)' — schema.org has no Company; the clean term is ours to mint. Business is NOT ADMITTED (same ruling): Company carries the referent.
